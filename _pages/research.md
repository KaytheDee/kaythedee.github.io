## Mentorship 
In the Summer of 2024, Ahana and I served as mentors as part of the UW-CEI Undergraduate Research Program. We mentored Lillian and Biniyam who were undergraduate students in Computer Science. 
Over the 8 weeks, they worked on conducting research on investigating mini-grid payments in Uganda, as well as inequities in access to heating and cooling infrastructure in the greater Seattle area.
